Curiosity, translated into concepts.

01 / COMPLETED PROTOTYPE

CerviSafe

CerviSafe is a cervical cancer awareness application prototype developed to explore how accessible information and digital tools could contribute to cervical cancer awareness and preventive health education.

The concept addresses the importance of accessible information surrounding cervical cancer, HPV vaccination, screening and the barriers that can affect awareness and access to preventive resources.

Project focus

The prototype explores educational resources, reminders related to preventive health, awareness information and the accessibility of relevant resources.

Contributions

Research, presentation and product development.

Development context

Developed as a prototype through Team MADIMA, exploring the intersection of health awareness, technology and accessible information.
